About RasterLab

RasterLab 2.0 is a GIS platform designed for spatial dataset normalization and matrix visualization. By rescaling raw array intensities to unit range $[0, 1]$, users can prepare remote sensing imagery (Landsat, Sentinel, DEMs) for machine learning models and spatial statistics without losing underlying georeferencing metadata.
